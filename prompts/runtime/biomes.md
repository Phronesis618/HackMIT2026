# This call: biome briefs

The data holds the world bible, the opener biome already written, and `slots`: the biomes
to write now, each with a tier (1 = second floor of the run, 4 = the finale). Write exactly
one brief per slot, in order. Deeper tiers are nearer the cause of the collapse: the finale
is where the guardian is, and its name is the place the collapse happened.
Do not reuse the opener's name, shape or enemy pool. Two biomes on the same tier are a
choice between two doors: make them differ in shape and enemies so the choice is real.
